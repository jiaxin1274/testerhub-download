GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is Tester Hub really free?

Yes — the current version is completely free to download and use, no account or license key required.

Does my data ever leave my computer?

No. Tester Hub runs a small local server on your own machine and opens it in your browser — recordings, screenshots, and reports are all stored locally. Nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you choose to use an external AI provider for descriptions.

Can my whole team share the same recordings?

Not yet in this version — each install keeps its own local data, so "team" features currently work per-machine rather than across the internet. Shared team workspaces are on the roadmap.

What happens on first launch?

Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" notice since this is an independently-published app — click More info → Run anyway to continue. This only appears once.

Is it Windows-only?

Yes, for now — Tester Hub relies on Windows-specific automation and screen-capture APIs.
